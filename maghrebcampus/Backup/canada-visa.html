IMM 5257 Application Form
Family Information Form (IMM 5645)

6 months bank statements
Employment letter (if employed)
Business registration (if self-employed)
Property ownership documents
Travel itinerary & flight reservation
Letter of invitation (if visiting family/friends)
Host's status in Canada (PR card/citizenship)
Proof of ties to Algeria (employment, family, property)
Previous travel history (passport stamps)
Purpose of travel letter
Marriage/birth certificates (if applicable)
Travel insurance (recommended)
